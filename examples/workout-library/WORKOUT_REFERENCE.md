All cycling templates have been removed.

### RUN_Z2_EASY — Aerobic Maintenance & Active Recovery
- **Objective:** Pure Zone 2 aerobic base maintenance, capillary building, and active recovery between heavy gym or interval days.
- **Structure:** 40 to 50 minutes continuous running strictly in Zone 2 (Heart Rate below AeT / ~75% HRmax).
- **Coaching Rules:** Low neuromuscular stress. If HR drifts into Zone 3, walk breaks are mandatory until HR returns to mid-Zone 2.

### RUN_Z2_LONG — Weekly Endurance Foundation
- **Objective:** Build mitochondrial density, fatigue resistance, and structural resilience for long-distance goals (Half Marathon+).
- **Structure:** 60 to 90 minutes continuous running strictly in Zone 2.
- **Coaching Rules:** Conversational pace (RPE 3-4). Must be scheduled with at least 24h separation from high-intensity work.

### RUN_TEMPO_CRUISE — Sub-Threshold Cruise Intervals
- **Objective:** Increase lactate drempel / threshold speed (4 Mijl, 5k, 10k pace) with controlled fatigue accumulation (Norwegian threshold approach).
- **Structure:**
  - 10 min Warm-up (Zone 1-2)
  - 3x 8 min Zone 3 / Threshold Pace (2 min easy jog/walk recovery)
  - 10 min Cool-down (Zone 1)
- **Coaching Rules:** **HARD SESSION.** Enforce strict 48-hour separation post-Lower Body gym session. Do NOT prescribe if TSB < -15 or ACWR > 1.3.

### RUN_TEMPO_SUSTAINED — Continuous Threshold Run
- **Objective:** Mental toughness, fatigue tolerance, and lactate clearance capacity at 10k to 15k race effort.
- **Structure:**
  - 12 min Warm-up (Zone 1-2)
  - 20 min Continuous Tempo Run in upper Zone 3 / Threshold
  - 10 min Cool-down (Zone 1)
- **Coaching Rules:** **HARD SESSION.** Enforce strict 48-hour separation post-Lower Body gym session.

### RUN_VO2_SHORT — 30/30 Micro-Intervals (Speed & Oxygen Kinetics)
- **Objective:** Maximize time spent at VO2max with reduced lactate buildup and lower neuromuscular wear compared to long intervals.
- **Structure:**
  - 12 min Warm-up (Zone 1-2) + 3 dynamic drills
  - 2 sets of 10x (30 sec hard Zone 4/5 effort / 30 sec easy jog recovery) with 3 min set rest
  - 10 min Cool-down (Zone 1)
- **Coaching Rules:** **HARD SESSION.** High central fatigue. Enforce strict 48-hour separation post-Lower Body gym session.

### RUN_VO2_CLASSIC — 4x 1km Classic Speed Intervals
- **Objective:** Build VO2max capacity, maximal aerobic speed (MAS), and top-end conditioning for short-distance dominance (5k / 10k).
- **Structure:**
  - 12 min Warm-up (Zone 1-2)
  - 4x 1000m at Zone 4/5 effort (2.5 min walking recovery between reps)
  - 10 min Cool-down (Zone 1)
- **Coaching Rules:** **HARD SESSION.** High mechanical fatigue. Strict 48-hour post-Lower Body gym rule applies.

### RUN_Z2_STRIDES — Aerobic Run with Neuromuscular Strides
- **Objective:** Enhance stride frequency, running economy, and turnover without triggering systemic fatigue.
- **Structure:**
  - 40 min Zone 2 Easy Run
  - 5x 100m Strides (smooth acceleration to 90% max speed, focus on relaxed form, full 60s walk recovery)
  - 5 min Cool-down
- **Coaching Rules:** Counts as an Easy Run (`is_hard_session: false`). Safe to schedule between gym days.
